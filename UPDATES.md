## October 2026

- **Product:** Kuaishou is rolling out Kling 4.0 in stages. Kling 4.0 Flash reached a limited group of
  members first (≈ 28 September); the full model is expected during October. Reported: clips of 3–30 s,
  10-bit HDR. [guide](https://pixverse.ai/en/blog/kling-4-release-date)
- **Industry:** Baseten added Runway Gen-4.5 and Aleph 2.0 for single-tenant enterprise deployment (14 October).
  [Unite.AI](https://unite.ai/baseten-adds-runways-gen-4-5-and-aleph-2-0-to-dedicated-inference)
- **Rankings:** Kling v3 leads one blind-vote text-to-video board; Google's Gemini Omni variants lead others.
  Boards disagree, so check them directly. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/leaderboards/best-ai-for-video-creation) ·
  [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/video/leaderboard/text-to-video)

## September 2026

- **Industry:** OpenAI's Sora API shut down on 24 September, completing the Sora exit.
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sora_%28text-to-video_model%29)
- **Industry:** A roundup names Seedance 2.5, MiniMax H3 and Wan 3 as the new leading trio.
  [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/09/07/ai-videos-new-power-trio-seedance-2-5-minimax-h3-wan-3/)
- **Research:** Interactive world models keep improving long-horizon memory, e.g.
  [WorldCrafter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24984) (implicit 3D-aware memory).

## August 2026

- **Product:** Alibaba opened the Wan 3.0 public beta (6 August): 30-second single-pass clips and
  document-to-video. It is hosted only; no open weights so far. [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/09/07/ai-videos-new-power-trio-seedance-2-5-minimax-h3-wan-3/)
- **Research:** [Matrix-Game 3.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29910) (real-time streaming world model with patch memory)
  and [Sekai2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09449) (from world exploration to interactive world modelling).

## July 2026

- **Open weights:** MiniMax released H3 as open weights (≈ 31 July). It ranks near the top of public boards,
  but its licence excludes the US, EU, UK and South Korea. [pinggy](https://pinggy.io/blog/best_video_generation_ai_models/)
- **Product:** ByteDance launched Seedance 2.5 to consumers on Dreamina (31 July): up to 30 s of output and
  up to 50 reference inputs. [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/09/07/ai-videos-new-power-trio-seedance-2-5-minimax-h3-wan-3/)

## June 2026

- **Product:** Gemini Omni Flash reached the Gemini API in public preview.
  [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-ai-updates-june-2026/)
- **Product:** ByteDance announced Seedance 2.5 at the Volcano Engine conference (23 June).
  [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/09/07/ai-videos-new-power-trio-seedance-2-5-minimax-h3-wan-3/)
- **World models:** NVIDIA announced Cosmos 3 at Computex (1 June).
  [Vizard](https://vizard.ai/blog/ai-video-news-june-2026-update-on-models-funding-platforms-and-what-comes-after-sora)

## May 2026

- **Product:** Google DeepMind announced Gemini Omni at I/O (19 May): one model family that takes text,
  image, audio and video and generates and edits video with sound. Combines Gemini with Veo, Nano Banana
  and Genie. SynthID watermark on by default. [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-gemini-omni-flash-video-model-io-2026)
- **Product:** Runway released Aleph 2 for video editing. [Unite.AI](https://unite.ai/baseten-adds-runways-gen-4-5-and-aleph-2-0-to-dedicated-inference)
- **Research:** Survey [Audio-Visual Intelligence in Large Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04045);
  benchmark [WBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25874) for multi-turn interactive world models.

## April 2026

- **Industry:** The Sora app closed on 26 April. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sora_%28text-to-video_model%29)
- **Product:** Veo 3.1 opened to anyone with a Google account through Google Vids, with 10 free generations
  a month (2 April). [Sunra](https://sunra.ai/blog/ai-video-generation-2026-sora-shutdown-veo-free-kling-viral)
- **Research:** Survey [Evolution of Video Generative Foundations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06339) (GAN → diffusion → AR → world models).

## March 2026

- **Industry:** OpenAI announced that it would shut down Sora, app and API (≈ 24 March), citing compute
  demand and a shift toward world-simulation research. [Engadget](https://engadget.com/ai/openai-is-shutting-down-its-sora-video-generation-app-211023358.html)
- **Product:** Google released Veo 3 Lite in the Gemini API (31 March): $0.05/s at 720p, $0.08/s at 1080p.
  [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ai-video-news-april-2026)
- **Industry:** ByteDance reportedly paused the global launch of Seedance 2.0 after copyright disputes with
  studios. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seedance_2.0)
- **World models:** Skywork released Matrix-Game 3.0. [Cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/frontier-gen-ai-models)

## February 2026

- **Product:** Kuaishou launched Kling 3.0 (≈ 4 February): Video 3.0 and Video 3.0 Omni, up to 15 s,
  native audio, better consistency. [press release](https://mrem.bernama.com/viewsm.php?idm=53441)
- **Product:** ByteDance launched Seedance 2.0 (12 February): one model for audio and video that accepts text,
  image, audio and video references. Viral celebrity clips drew objections from film studios.
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seedance_2.0)
- **Product:** Google redesigned Flow, its filmmaking tool (≈ 25 February). [post](https://x.com/hardeep_gambhir/status/2029069826078154930)

## January 2026

- **Open weights:** Lightricks published the LTX-2 report, an open model that generates audio and video
  together. [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03233)
- **World models:** Google DeepMind opened Project Genie to AI Ultra subscribers (≈ 29 January).
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genie_%28AI_model%29)
- **Research:** [A Mechanistic View on Video Generation as World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17067) and
  [Robotic Video World Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07823).

## December 2025

- **Product:** Runway released Gen-4.5 (1 December). It debuted at #1 on the Artificial Analysis
  text-to-video board (1,247 Elo). [Runway](https://runway.com/research/introducing-runway-gen-4.5)
- **Research:** [BAgger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12080) (reduces drift in autoregressive video diffusion) and
  [VABench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09299) (benchmark for video with synchronised audio).

## November 2025

- **Open weights:** Tencent released HunyuanVideo 1.5 (8.3B parameters). [report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.18870)
- **World models:** World Labs shipped Marble, a 3D world generator. [Spheron](https://www.spheron.network/blog/gpu-infrastructure-world-models-2026/)
- **Research:** [Simulating the Visual World with Artificial Intelligence: A Roadmap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08585).

## October 2025

- **Product:** Google released Veo 3.1 (15 October): richer audio, object-level editing, and "Ingredients to
  Video" and "Frames to Video" modes in Flow. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veo_%28text-to-video_model%29) ·
  [newsletter](https://heatherbcooper.substack.com/p/ai-video-wars-include-veo-31-sora)

## September 2025

- **Product:** OpenAI launched Sora 2 with the Sora social app (≈ 30 September), including Cameo
  self-insertion and Remix. [newsletter](https://heatherbcooper.substack.com/p/ai-video-wars-include-veo-31-sora)
- **Product:** Kling 2.5 Turbo (faster, cheaper, more consistent), Luma Ray3 (reasoning and HDR video) and
  Wan 2.5 (hosted, no open weights). [newsletter](https://heatherbcooper.substack.com/p/ai-video-wars-include-veo-31-sora)
- **Research:** [Video models are zero-shot learners and reasoners](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20328) (Veo 3 solves many
  vision tasks it was never trained for).

## August 2025

- **World models:** Google DeepMind announced Genie 3 (5 August): real-time interactive worlds at 720p,
  24 fps, consistent for minutes. Not open. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genie_%28AI_model%29)
- **Product:** xAI launched Grok Imagine (4 August): text or image to video with native audio.
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/04/grok-imagine-xais-new-ai-image-and-video-generator-lets-you-make-nsfw-content/)
- **Open weights:** Skywork released Matrix-Game 2.0 (12 August), an open real-time interactive world model.
  [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/matrix-game-2-0-launches-as-a-powerful-open-source-alternative-to-genie-3--302527562.html)

## July 2025

- **Product:** Runway introduced Aleph (25 July), an in-context video model that adds, removes and changes
  objects, angles, style and lighting in an existing video. [Runway](https://runway.com/research/introducing-runway-aleph)
- **Open weights:** Alibaba released Wan 2.2 (Apache 2.0): a 27B mixture-of-experts model with 14B active
  parameters; its 5B variant runs at 720p on one RTX 4090. [pinggy](https://pinggy.io/blog/best_video_generation_ai_models/)
